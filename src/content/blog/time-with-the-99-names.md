99 Names – Asmaul Husna is a calm place to learn and return to the beautiful names of Allah, one at a time.

## Arabic, transliteration and meaning

Each name shows the Arabic, its transliteration, and its meaning in English and Urdu, so you can read and understand it together.

## Reflect and keep favourites

Read a short reflection on a name, and save the ones you want to come back to.

## Always with you

Everything works fully offline, so the names are there whether or not you have a connection.
