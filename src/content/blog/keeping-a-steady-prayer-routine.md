A routine grows from small, steady steps. SalahLog helps you notice them by keeping a simple record of your daily prayers.

## Record each prayer

Mark each of the five daily prayers as you pray them. It takes a moment and keeps your day in view.

## Look back on your progress

Your prayer history shows how your practice is going over time, so you can see where you are steady and where you want to grow.

## Times and reminders

Check prayer times and set up reminders for each prayer to support your routine.
