Most budgets fail at the same step: writing things down. Budget AI makes that step as quick as sending a message.

## Three ways to log

Type an expense in a chat, say it out loud, or take a photo of the receipt. Income works the same way.

## See where it goes

Browse your entries and explore spending insights to understand your patterns. Home Screen widgets keep your totals one glance away.

## What is free

Manual tracking is free. AI features are paid, with 5 free AI messages each month.
