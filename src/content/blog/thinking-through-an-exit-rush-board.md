Exit Rush looks simple: tap an arrow and it slides along its path. The puzzle is that arrows block each other, so the order you tap them in decides everything.

## Look before you tap

Find the arrows with a clear path to the edge first. Clearing them often opens the way for the rest of the board.

## Protect your three hearts

A wrong move costs a heart, and you only have three. Slow down on crowded boards and keep an eye on the timer.

## Try the special stages

When you want something different, special stages change up the challenge. Exit Rush also plays offline, so a quick board fits anywhere.
