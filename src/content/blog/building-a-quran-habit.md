Finishing the Quran can feel like a big goal. Quran Khatam Tracker breaks it into the smallest step there is: one ayah. Mark it, and you have moved forward.

## Begin with a niyyah

Every journey starts with an intention. Set yours, and the app keeps it in front of you as you read.

## Mark each ayah

Read and mark ayah by ayah. When you come back, the app takes you straight to where you stopped, so there is no hunting for your place.

## Watch your khatam grow

Your progress builds into khatam journeys. Reading streaks and awards give you a gentle reason to return tomorrow.
