Most days are full. Rafeeq was made for the small gaps in between: a few minutes before work, a pause after Maghrib, the quiet before sleep. It brings the things you reach for most into one place, so you spend less time searching and more time on what matters.

## Read and listen to the Quran

Open a surah, read at your own pace, or play the recitation and follow along. Each ayah is laid out clearly, so it is easy to pick up where you left off.

## Know when and where to pray

Prayer times sit on the home screen with a countdown to the next one, and the Qibla is a tap away when you are somewhere new.

## Duas and a Namaz guide, close at hand

Keep your daily duas nearby and use the Namaz guide when you want to check a step. Rafeeq supports English and Urdu.
